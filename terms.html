In short

Project scope, quotes and pricing

Every project is quoted individually. After a short discovery conversation about your goals, your content and what the website or application needs to do, I send a written quote and scope. The quote describes what will be built, what it costs, how payment works, an estimated timeline, and what revisions are included. No work begins until you have accepted the quote.

Prices

Starting points are published on the pricing page: professional websites start at $300, and most standard website projects fall between $300 and $700 depending on size, pages, features, integrations and complexity. Web applications, dashboards, API integrations, e-commerce systems and other larger or complex work are quoted individually. A starting point is not a quote: every project is priced for the project at hand, all prices are in US dollars, and the quote you accept is what applies.

A quote describes a specific project at a specific time. If the scope changes, or if you come back later with a new project, a new quote applies.

Work outside the agreed scope

Requests that go beyond the agreed scope (new pages, features or integrations that were not in the quote, or a change of direction) are quoted separately before the work is done. Minor changes may already be covered if you have a website care plan (see Website care plans).

Payment

Payment terms are set in your quote. That includes whether a deposit is required, any milestone payments, when payments are due, and which payment methods are accepted. If anything about payment is unclear, ask before accepting the quote; I would rather sort it out up front.

Ownership of the finished website passes to you when the project is paid in full (see Ownership and intellectual property).

Your domain stays yours. Clients purchase and maintain ownership of their domain registration. You register the domain in your own name so it stays under your control, and I can help you connect it to your new website.

Domain registration and renewal costs are not included in the website development price unless specifically agreed otherwise in your quote.

Hosting and third-party costs

Some things a website needs come from other providers. Hosting, paid APIs, commercial plugins, SaaS subscriptions and specialty services are separate from Light Astra's fees unless specifically agreed otherwise in your quote. Where a project uses such services, the account and the relationship with the provider are yours. Light Astra is not responsible for a provider's pricing, availability, changes or outages.

Revisions

Your quote states what revisions are included in the project, so you know up front how much back-and-forth is part of the price. Additional revision rounds, or a change of direction after you have approved a design or a stage of the work, are quoted separately.

No project or care plan includes unlimited revisions.

Website care plans

Once a website is live, Light Astra can look after it for you through optional monthly plans: Astra Care ($10/month), basic website care and maintenance, and Astra Plus ($39/month), which adds security-focused care, technical SEO and Google indexing support, monitoring and priority support. Both are optional. The plan you choose, and the site or sites it covers, is confirmed in writing with your quote or when you sign up.

Astra Care covers routine website checks, basic updates, minor content changes, link checks, basic maintenance, backup checks where configured, a general website health review and assistance when minor problems occur. Astra Plus includes everything in Astra Care plus security-focused maintenance, configuration reviews, HTTPS/SSL checks, dependency and software updates where applicable, basic uptime monitoring, backup checks, broken link checks, performance checks, Google Search Console support, sitemap monitoring and submission assistance, indexing checks, technical SEO maintenance, small content adjustments and priority support. The current inclusions of each plan are listed on the pricing page.

Security-focused maintenance is designed to reduce common website risks. No website or security service can guarantee prevention of every cyberattack or compromise. See Security limitations.

Business application and internal-system projects are quoted separately and may use separate operation, support or service plans, as described in the applicable quote, agreement or current pricing information. Those plans are not website care plans, and recurring services are always separate from the initial one-time project or development fee.

How the plans work

  • Billing. Plans are billed monthly, in US dollars. Which site or sites the plan covers, the start date and the payment method are confirmed when you sign up.
  • Routine care only. Plans cover routine care, not unlimited work. They do not include redesigns or new development; major redesigns and new development are separate projects, quoted separately (see Major redesigns and new development).
  • Third-party costs. Hosting, domain registration, paid APIs, plugins, SaaS subscriptions and other third-party costs are separate from the plan price (see Hosting and third-party costs).
  • Cancellation. Either side can cancel a plan with notice as stated when you sign up. After cancellation, the plan ends at the end of the current billing period.
