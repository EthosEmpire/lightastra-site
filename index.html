How it works

A straightforward way to get a website you're proud of.

No fixed packages and no pressure: every project is quoted individually, and you talk to the developer who builds your site from the first message to launch.

  1. 01

    Tell me about your project

    A few sentences are enough to start: your goals, what the site should do, and anything you already have.

  2. 02

    A short conversation

    I read your request and reply by email. A short conversation clarifies what you need and what would be the simplest solution.

  3. 03

    A clear proposal

    You receive a proposal with scope, quote and timeline estimate. Prices are in US dollars, and the quote you accept is what applies.

  4. 04

    Build and launch

    Once you accept the proposal the work begins, and you stay in direct contact with the person building your site until it launches.
